Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 2026 · OxladeAIAutos

OxladeAIAutos ("we," "our," or "us") provides AI automation and workflow integration services, including automated systems that connect with messaging platforms such as Instagram and WhatsApp on behalf of our business clients. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and how we protect it.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on the service being provided, we may collect:

  • Messaging data: messages, sender identifiers, and conversation metadata received through connected channels such as Instagram and WhatsApp, for the purpose of organizing and tracking customer inquiries on behalf of our clients.
  • Contact information: names, usernames, phone numbers, or other identifiers provided through these messaging channels.
  • Payment information: collected only at the point a customer converts into a paying client, and processed through third-party payment providers rather than stored directly by us wherever possible.
  • Technical data: basic system and workflow execution information (such as timestamps and delivery identifiers) used to ensure messages are processed reliably and without duplication.

2. How We Use Information

Information collected is used only to:

  • Deliver the automation and workflow services our clients have engaged us to build and operate;
  • Organize, store, and track incoming customer conversations on behalf of a client business;
  • Provide technical support and maintain system reliability;
  • Process payments when a customer converts to a paying client.

We do not sell customer data, and we do not use collected messaging data to independently contact or market to customers outside the scope of the service a client has engaged us for.

3. Data Storage and Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information we handle, including:

  • Restricting internal access to stored data;
  • Using secure authentication for systems and integrations;
  • Encrypting sensitive credentials and access tokens;
  • Maintaining audit trails so that data handling can be reviewed and verified.

4. Sharing of Information

We do not share collected data with third parties except:

  • With the client business the data was collected on behalf of;
  • With service providers strictly necessary to operate our systems (such as hosting or payment processing providers), under appropriate confidentiality obligations;
  • Where required by law.

5. Use of Meta Platforms (Instagram & WhatsApp)

Where our systems connect to Instagram or WhatsApp on behalf of a client business, data is accessed only through Meta's official APIs and in accordance with Meta's platform policies. Message data received through these channels is used solely to support the client business's own customer communications and lead management — it is not used for any unrelated purpose, and automated systems do not independently send marketing messages to customers without the client's direction.

6. Data Retention

Information is retained only as long as necessary to provide the relevant service to our client, or as required by applicable law. Clients may request deletion of stored data associated with their business, subject to any legal retention requirements.

7. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, or request deletion of your personal information. If you are a customer of a business that uses our systems and have questions about your data, you may contact us using the details below, or contact that business directly.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or for legal and regulatory reasons. The effective date above indicates when this policy was last updated.
